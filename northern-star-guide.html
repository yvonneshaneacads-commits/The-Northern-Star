✦ What if you didn’t need to have your whole life figured out? ✦

Somewhere between chasing milestones, meeting expectations, and trying to become the person we dream of being, we forget that we’re allowed to grow at our own pace. That thought inspired The Northern Star. ✧

A little corner of Notion for fellow travelers who are still figuring things out. A space to pause, reflect, rediscover what matters, and take small steps toward a life that feels like their own.

  • ☆ Guided reflections to understand yourself better
  • ☆ A space to explore your values, expectations, and self-doubt
  • ☆ Goal-setting tools to chart your own course
  • ☆ Habit trackers and small steps to turn intentions into action
  • ☆ Progress logs to celebrate the little things

And the best part? It’s free. ♡
